MCD Explorer

The MCD format interpretation in src/mcd.js is adapted from readimc:
https://github.com/BodenmillerGroup/readimc
In particular, mcd_file.py and mcd_parser.py informed XML discovery,
acquisition reconstruction, channel metadata, and embedded image offsets.
Interface colours and interaction patterns follow SBT IMC Explorer.
